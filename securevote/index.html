How SecureVote Works

A 5-layer verification pipeline that ensures only authenticated, eligible voters can participate.

01

Voter Registration

Voters register with government ID and biometric data. The system assigns a unique cryptographic identity reference.

KYCAadhar/IDBiometric
02

Live Biometric Check

Real-time facial recognition with liveness detection (blinking, head motion) prevents deepfake and spoofing attacks.

LivenessFaceIDAnti-Spoof
03

Session ID Validation

Per-session government ID upload is cross-validated against live image. No permanent storage of sensitive documents.

OCRSession-basedEncrypted
04

Trust Score Engine

AI computes a composite trust score from facial match, behavioral analysis, device fingerprint, and location consistency.

Anonymous Vote Cast

Vote is anonymized, encrypted, and submitted. All biometric data is securely purged immediately after submission.

Security at Every Layer

Biometric Verification

Active Protection

Behavioral Analysis

Keystroke dynamics, touch patterns, and navigation behavior are analyzed to distinguish humans from bots and fake profiles.

AI Monitoring

Device Fingerprinting
